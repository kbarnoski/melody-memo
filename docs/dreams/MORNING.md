## New since yesterday — consonance you can SEE, drawn in living SVG
- **[16944-nodalfigure](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/16944-nodalfigure)** — *SEE the consonance of your chords as a single drawn figure.* Press play. The centre of the screen holds one evolving **harmonograph** — a pendulum-drawn curve whose frequency ratios ARE the just-intonation intervals of the chord sounding in your recording right now. **When your harmony is consonant, the figure locks into a clean closed loop; when it tenses (a 7♭9, an altered dominant), it frays into a slowly-drifting open web** — and a live "consonance %" + "closing / drifting" label tells you which.
